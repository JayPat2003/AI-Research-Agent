# GLP-1 Receptor Agonist Outcomes Trials

GLP-1 receptor agonists lower glucose and body weight. A subset of large CVOTs also showed reductions in 3-point MACE in people with type 2 diabetes at high cardiovascular risk.

## Diabetes CVOTs

LEADER (liraglutide) reduced 3-point MACE in a high-risk type 2 diabetes population. SUSTAIN-6 (once-weekly injectable semaglutide) likewise reduced MACE, with additional attention on retinopathy complications in a small high-risk subgroup that needs cautious interpretation rather than slogan-level claims. REWIND (dulaglutide) enrolled a broader primary- plus secondary-prevention mix and showed a MACE reduction. HARMONY Outcomes (albiglutide) was positive for MACE but the product was later withdrawn for commercial reasons, which matters if an analyst confuses historical efficacy with currently marketed options. EXSCEL (once-weekly exenatide) was closer to a neutral MACE result, again warning against class-wide copy-paste.

PIONEER-6 seeded cardiovascular safety for oral semaglutide; SOUL later evaluated oral semaglutide for superiority on MACE in diabetes with CVD or CKD. FLOW evaluated injectable semaglutide for a kidney composite in T2D with CKD and is the key GLP-1 renal-outcomes citation in many 2024–2025 briefings.

## Obesity without diabetes

SELECT tested once-weekly semaglutide 2.4 mg in people with overweight or obesity and established CVD but without diabetes, and reported a reduction in 3-point MACE. That trial changed how analysts talk about GLP-1s: the cardiovascular story is no longer confined to antihyperglycemic use.

## Dual agonists

Tirzepatide (GIP/GLP-1) produced large glycemic and weight effects in SURPASS and SURMOUNT programs. SURPASS-CVOT is the dedicated cardiovascular outcomes comparison (often versus dulaglutide) and should be described as ongoing or read-out depending on the briefing date, never invented.

## Practical trade-offs

GLP-1 RAs typically outperform SGLT2 inhibitors for weight loss and often for A1c. Gastrointestinal adverse events (nausea, vomiting, early discontinuation) are the main persistence problem. They do not substitute for SGLT2 inhibitors when the primary goal is HF hospitalization or CKD progression unless dedicated evidence for that agent and indication exists.

For a production briefing aimed at clinical operations or medical affairs, GLP-1 RAs are usually favored when obesity, atherosclerotic risk, and glycemic potency co-dominate, provided supply, cost, and injection/oral preference are in scope.
