# SGLT2 Inhibitor Cardiovascular and Kidney Outcomes Trials

SGLT2 inhibitors were originally developed for glucose lowering. Large CVOTs then showed that several members of the class reduce hard cardiorenal events, including in some people without type 2 diabetes.

## Diabetes with atherosclerotic risk

EMPA-REG OUTCOME (empagliflozin) enrolled adults with type 2 diabetes and established cardiovascular disease and showed a reduction in 3-point MACE, with a marked reduction in cardiovascular death and heart-failure hospitalization relative to placebo on top of usual care. CANVAS (canagliflozin) and DECLARE-TIMI 58 (dapagliflozin) extended the class story: MACE benefit was clearer in higher-baseline ASCVD populations, while HF hospitalization and kidney outcomes were more consistent across trials. VERTIS CV (ertugliflozin) was closer to non-inferiority than superiority for MACE, which is a reminder not to treat every class member as interchangeable for atherosclerotic endpoints.

## Kidney disease

CREDENCE (canagliflozin in T2D with albuminuric CKD), DAPA-CKD (dapagliflozin in CKD with or without T2D), and EMPA-KIDNEY (empagliflozin in CKD) showed slowing of kidney disease progression and reductions in related clinical events. These trials are the backbone of SGLT2 use in nephrology briefings.

## Heart failure, with or without diabetes

DAPA-HF and EMPEROR-Reduced demonstrated benefit in reduced-ejection-fraction HF. EMPEROR-Preserved and DELIVER extended benefit into HF with mildly reduced or preserved ejection fraction. A large share of enrolled patients did not have type 2 diabetes, which is why SGLT2 inhibitors are now framed as HF drugs, not only diabetes drugs.

## Safety themes for briefings

Class safety discussions typically cover genital mycotic infections, volume depletion, rare euglycemic ketoacidosis, and a canagliflozin-associated amputation signal observed in CANVAS that did not generalize cleanly to every later trial. Analysts should state the trial, not the rumor.

For production decision support (coverage policy, pathway design, or medical-affairs Q&A), SGLT2 inhibitors are usually the first evidence-backed add-on when HF or CKD dominates the indication. They are oral and have less gastrointestinal attrition than GLP-1 RAs, at the cost of smaller average weight loss.
